About this model & sources +

CatBoost reproduced from 1,320 public records (Thomas et al., 2025). Inverse design searches six metals on a 1 nm grid, prioritizing tolerance, then equal-weight T/R/A RMSE.

Film conditions are fixed. Outputs are predictions, not measurements; values may fall outside 0–100% or not sum to 100%. Tolerance is a design requirement.
